Both batches completed 256/256 tests successfully. Every logged evolution fitness, optimization fitness, and evolution generation count matches across runs. Settings snapshots match each other and the checked-in source. No integrity, missing-test, success-marker/status, or cumulative-generation discrepancy was found.

Paired Durations are identical at the logged resolution on 223 tests; Run 2 is faster on 16 and slower on 17. This equality of rounded means is not a claim that every unrounded elapsed time was identical. Final cumulative solution times are 42.35 and 42.31 seconds; the final saved-state application Run durations are 42.685173 and 42.649148 seconds.

Test 0 remains included: 0.18 seconds over two recorded generations, GenTime 0.09 in Run 1; 0.14 seconds over two generations, GenTime 0.07 in Run 2. The startup difference is observed, not corrected or removed. Rebooting does not establish an empty persistent cache; these are the first and second launches in the user's manual sequence. No cold-cache or fresh-compilation claim is made, and no cache was manipulated.

Generation-average timing remains approximately flat, with small test-to-test variation and no persistent step slowdown. The chronological quarter means of logged GenTime are 0.06422, 0.06344, 0.06266, 0.06266 seconds in Run 1 and 0.06172, 0.06125, 0.06016, 0.06078 in Run 2. Median logged GenTime is 0.06 seconds in both. Observed logged values span 0.05–0.09 and 0.05–0.08 seconds. Eight chronological windows are retained in analysis.json.

`GenTime` is unrounded whole-test Duration divided by the evolution state's generation count, then printed to 0.01 seconds. It includes solution search and optimization overlap; it is not a trace of each evolution generation or a kernel timer. Dividing the already rounded Duration by Generation follows a different rounding path: means 0.0614437 and 0.0614476 seconds, median 0.055 in both. A Duration printed as 0.11 over two generations can coexist with GenTime printed as 0.05 or 0.06. The approximately 0.00227-second difference in mean *printed* GenTime is sensitive to that rounding and is not evidence of a substantial second-run improvement. The fitted Duration/Generation change across each batch is about −0.0031 seconds, with weak correlations around −0.10.

Descriptive high-duration outliers are tests 120, 221, 230, 242 in both runs. The maximum, test 221, is 0.63 seconds over eight generations in both. These longer searches are retained in all statistics. Generation-average high outliers are isolated and listed in analysis.md; they do not become a persistent timing shift. No unexpected evolutionary divergence is present.

Only final test 255's saved-state precision is available: maximum absolute error 0.00000069 on 256 samples, versus fitness 0.00000006 on 15 samples, in both runs. The other tests' dense precision is not logged. No complete per-test precision success rate or direct CPU/GPU arithmetic validation can be inferred.
